 ariglad connects you with employees when they need you most
 never leave Slack
 If an employee has a problem or question for HR, they can easily submit an anonymous case through Slack.
 HR will be notified immediately and will be able to communicate with the employee in real-time, either through Slack or the ariglad portal.
 support made easy
